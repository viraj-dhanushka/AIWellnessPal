About AI Wellness Pal

AI Wellness Pal is a free AI wellness coach app for iPhone and Android. Each day a 30-second check-in (energy, mood, how your body feels and how much time you have) becomes Today’s Plan: two to four small, doable actions for mood, sleep, movement and nutrition. An AI companion, the Wellness Pal, helps you follow through, alongside a journal, habit tracking and nine everyday wellness tools.

Why AI Wellness Pal exists

Most wellness apps hand you the same plan as everyone else. AI Wellness Pal asks how you’re actually doing first. Every morning a 30-second check-in captures your energy, your mood, how your body feels and how much time you genuinely have. Then your Pal builds Today’s Plan: two to four small, doable actions shaped around that answer. Exhausted with fifteen minutes free? You get a very different day than when you’re rested with an hour.

Everything else in the app supports that daily plan: tools to track meals, water, sleep, cycles and weight, a habit builder with challenges, a guided journal, and a Wellness Pal you can chat or talk with when you need it.

Who builds it

AI Wellness Pal is designed and built by Viraj Dhanushka, an independent developer. Feedback goes straight to the person who builds the app, and shapes what comes next. Send ideas, bugs or praise through the feedback form or by email.

How the AI is used, and its limits

The AI features use Google’s Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic. Before any of your information is sent to the AI, the app shows you what will be shared and asks for your permission. AI responses, calorie estimates and cycle predictions can be wrong; AI Wellness Pal provides general wellness information and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional care.

Privacy commitments

  • No advertising, no selling of data, and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Health data is read only with your permission (sleep only) and never written back.
  • Delete your account and data at any time, in the app or on the web.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
