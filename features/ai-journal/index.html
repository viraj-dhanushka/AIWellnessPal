Reflection · AI journal

An AI journal that asks the right questions

Guided reflections in AI Wellness Pal give you prompts based on how your week has actually gone. Write in your own words and your Wellness Pal responds with something supportive and specific.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

Prompts from your real week

A blank page is hard. Start a reflection and you get prompts drawn from your recent check-ins, energy and sleep, like:

  • “What’s weighing on my mind today?”
  • “How have my sleep and energy been lately?”
  • “What would help me feel more balanced right now?”

Responses that are specific, not generic

Write your entry and your Wellness Pal responds with something supportive and specific to what you wrote, not a stock affirmation. Over time your reflections help you spot patterns and see what actually helps you feel better.

Go deeper with Premium

Open any reflection and tap Discuss to talk it through properly with your Pal, then save anything useful that comes out of it as an Insight you can come back to. Write three or more reflections in a week and Premium also gives you a Weekly Reflection summary.

Turn conversations into reflections

Had a good conversation with your Pal? Tap “Generate Reflection” and it becomes a journal entry, so the thinking you did isn’t lost.

Private by design

Your journal is stored in your own private area of your account and isn’t shared with other users. AI Wellness Pal has no ads and doesn’t sell your data. When a reflection is sent to the AI for a response, the app asks your permission first.

FAQ

AI journal & reflections questions

Is the AI journal free?

Yes. Guided reflections and AI responses are part of the free app. Premium adds “Discuss”, a full conversation about any reflection, and a Weekly Reflection summary.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Entries are stored in your own private area and never shared with other users, and the app has no ads and doesn’t sell data. See the Privacy Policy.

What is an Insight?

Something useful you save while discussing a reflection with your Pal, so you can revisit it later.
