How it works

One check-in. A plan that fits today.

Most wellness apps hand you the same plan as everyone else. AI Wellness Pal asks how you're actually doing first.

  1. Check in (30 seconds)

    Rate your energy, pick your mood, and say how your body feels and how much time you have. Skip anything you like.

  2. Get Today's Plan

    Two to four small, doable actions shaped around your answers. Exhausted with fifteen minutes free? You get a very different day than when you're rested with an hour.

  3. Follow through with your Pal

    Tick actions off, track water, meals, sleep and habits, and talk things through with your AI Wellness Pal when you need it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is AI Wellness Pal?

AI Wellness Pal is a free AI wellness coach app for iPhone and Android. It answers one question each day: what should I do for my wellbeing right now?

You do a quick check-in, and the app builds Today’s Plan: two to four small, doable actions shaped around your energy, mood, body recovery and how much time you actually have. Everything else in the app supports that: tools to track the day, a Wellness Pal to talk it through, and progress you can see building up over time.

Is AI Wellness Pal free?

Yes. AI Wellness Pal is free to download and use on iPhone and Android, with no ads. The free app includes daily check-ins and Today’s Plan, the AI journal, habits and challenges, hydration, sleep, period and weight tracking, meditation and breathing sessions, widgets and 3 Wellness Pal chat messages a day.

An optional Premium subscription adds unlimited chat, real-time voice conversations, AI food analysis, AI meal plans and the other AI coaching inside the toolkit. Compare Free and Premium.

How much does AI Wellness Pal Premium cost?

On the US App Store, Premium costs $14.99 a month or $89.99 a year, and both plans start with a 7-day free trial. Prices vary by country and currency; the App Store or Google Play shows the exact price before you confirm. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Is AI Wellness Pal available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download it from the App Store (iPhone, iOS 15.0 or later) or Google Play (Android 8.0 or later). Sign in with the same account on both and your data syncs.

What is the daily check-in?

Three quick steps: your energy (1–5), your mood, and how your body feels plus the time you have today. You can skip any step and the plan uses what you gave it. Once you’ve checked in, Today’s Plan is generated within a few seconds. You can also ask your Wellness Pal to “start my check-in” if you’d rather talk than tap.

Is AI Wellness Pal medical advice?

No. AI Wellness Pal offers general wellness information and encouragement. It isn’t a medical device and doesn’t diagnose, treat or replace a doctor, therapist or other qualified professional. AI responses, calorie estimates and cycle predictions can be wrong, so talk to a healthcare provider about any health concern.

If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number right away. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK, call 111 or Samaritans on 116 123. Elsewhere, find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Which AI does AI Wellness Pal use?

The AI features (Wellness Pal chat and voice, journal insights, daily plans, sleep insights and nutrition analysis) are powered by Google’s Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic. Before any of your information is sent to the AI, the app shows you what will be shared and asks for your permission. AI responses can be inaccurate, so use them as guidance, not fact.

Is my data private? Do you sell it or show ads?

AI Wellness Pal has no ads, doesn’t sell your data and doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Your data is stored in your own private area and isn’t shared with other users. Data is shared only with the service providers that run the app (such as Firebase for storage and Google Gemini for AI responses). Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Support

Here to help

Need help, found a bug, or have a question? Email aiwellnesspal@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you. You can also report a bug on GitHub.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your data. No longer have the app? Request deletion on the web.

Manage or cancel Premium

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → AI Wellness Pal. On Android, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. To restore Premium on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app.

Connect or disconnect sleep data

On iPhone, go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → AI Wellness Pal. On Android, open Health Connect → App permissions → AI Wellness Pal.

Not medical advice

AI Wellness Pal offers general wellness information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you are in crisis or have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.
