AI Wellness Pal

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Wellness Pal (“the app”, “we”, “us”), published by Viraj Dhanushka, collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the app on iOS or Android.

The short version
  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account, the wellness data you enter, and optional health, photo and voice input.
  • AI features send the relevant content to Google’s Gemini models to generate a response.
  • Friends and the Wellness Planet are optional. If you use them, other people can see your username, display name, your garden and the country you pick. Your journal, check-ins, health data and conversations are never shown to other people.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and, if you provide it, your name and profile photo. You can sign up with email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In. If you use Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address, in which case we receive an Apple relay address instead.

Wellness content you enter

This includes daily check-ins (energy, mood, recovery), wellness intentions and plans, journal entries and reflections, habits and challenges, hydration logs, meals and nutrition goals, period and weight tracking entries, wellness library progress and bookmarks, journey milestones, your wellness coins, level, Wellness Garden and rewards, and your conversations with the Wellness Pal.

Friends and the Wellness Planet (optional)

These features are off until you choose to use them.

  • Friend profile. To add friends you create a friend profile with a username and a display name. Your profile also shows the emoji of your garden companion. Any signed-in user of the app can find your profile by searching for your username or display name, and can send you a friend request.
  • Friend requests and friends. We store the requests you send and receive and the friends you accept. Only you and the other person can see a request or a friendship.
  • Your shared garden. Friends you accept can see your Wellness Garden: how many days you have shown up, the plants, decorations and companion you have, and the garden visitors you have met. Your mood flowers are shown to friends only if you turn on mood sharing; otherwise friends see a neutral flower. Friends never see your coins, journal, check-ins, health data, meals or conversations.
  • The Wellness Planet. If you join the planet, a public entry is stored with the country you pick, your garden’s place on the globe, how many days you have shown up and how many flowers are blooming. Any signed-in user of the app can see it. Your username, display name and companion emoji are added only if you choose to show your profile on the planet. The country is the one you pick, not your device’s location.
  • Blocking and reporting. If you block someone, we store that so they can no longer see your garden or send you requests. If you report someone, we store your report (who you reported, the reason you chose, the date, and their username and display name at that time) so we can review it and act on it, including removing an account that breaks our Terms of Use.

You can stop sharing at any time: turn off mood sharing, hide your profile on the planet, leave the planet, remove a friend, block someone, or delete your friend profile, which removes your username, your friendships and your shared garden.

Health data (Apple Health and Health Connect)

If you choose to connect Apple Health (on iPhone) or Health Connect (on Android), the app reads your sleep data to show your sleep score, sleep stages and recovery insights. The app does not read any other health data and does not write any data to Apple Health or Health Connect. Daily sleep summaries (total sleep time, stages and score) and the AI insights generated from them are stored in your account so your history is available across devices.

Data obtained from Apple HealthKit or Health Connect is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties except as needed to provide the features described here (see “AI processing” below).

Our use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Google Play Android Health Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Photos and camera

With your permission, you can take or choose photos for your profile picture, for journey memories, and for meals you want the AI Nutrition Coach to analyse. Meal and memory photos you upload are stored in your account.

Microphone and speech

With your permission, the microphone is used for voice commands and live voice conversations with the Wellness Pal. Speech recognition may be processed by your device’s operating system provider (Apple or Google). During a live voice conversation, audio is streamed to Google’s Gemini service to generate spoken replies. We store the text transcript of a conversation in your account; we do not store the audio recording.

Location you choose

The app does not access your device’s GPS location. If you search for a place (for example, to get weather-based hydration advice or add a journey memory), the place name you enter is sent to a geocoding service to find it. When you join the Wellness Planet, the name of the country you pick is sent to Mapbox to find where it is on the globe.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Premium, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We receive your subscription status and purchase history (not your payment card details) through RevenueCat, which we use to manage subscriptions.

Device and notification information

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled and shown by your device itself. No push notification service is used, and nothing about your reminders is sent to us or to a third party. We use Firebase App Check, which verifies that requests come from a genuine copy of the app on a real device. The map component (Mapbox) and Google Firebase may collect anonymous usage, performance and diagnostic data about the app, such as how the map is used and error reports. This data is not linked to your account.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app’s features, such as personalised daily plans, reflections, sleep and nutrition insights, and Wellness Pal conversations.
  • To sync your data securely across your devices.
  • To manage your account and Premium subscription.
  • To send reminders and notifications you have chosen to receive.
  • If you use Friends or the Wellness Planet, to let you find friends, share your garden with them, and show your garden on the planet.
  • To review reports about other users and act on them.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To review AI responses you report as harmful, inaccurate or inappropriate, and to improve safety.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

3. AI processing

AI features in the app (Wellness Pal chat and voice, journal insights, daily plans, sleep insights and nutrition analysis) are powered by Google’s Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic and Google Cloud. When you use these features, the content needed for that request, such as your message, a recent conversation, a journal entry, a meal photo or description, or a summary of your check-ins and sleep, is sent to Google to generate a response. Google processes this data on our behalf under its terms for these services.

Before any of your information is sent to Google Gemini, the app shows you what will be shared and asks for your permission. The app’s features are built on AI, so if you don’t agree you can sign out and come back at any time.

AI responses can be inaccurate. They are for general wellness information only and are not medical advice. See our Terms of Use.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and the data stored with it, including your wellness content, conversations, sleep and meal records, uploaded photos, coins and rewards, and your friend profile, username, friendships, shared garden, block list and Wellness Planet entry. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app’s access to your Apple ID.

Some data may remain for a short period in backups or with our service providers before it is removed under their standard retention schedules.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules ensure that only your signed-in account can read or write your data. The only exceptions are the things you choose to share: your friend profile and Wellness Planet entry can be read by signed-in users, and your shared garden by the friends you accept and haven’t blocked. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit most of your data directly in the app.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and data in the app at any time.
  • Sharing: you can turn off mood sharing, hide your profile on the planet, leave the planet, remove or block people, or delete your friend profile in the app at any time.
  • Permissions: you can turn Apple Health, Health Connect, camera, photos, microphone, speech recognition and notification access on or off at any time in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as requesting a copy of your data or objecting to certain processing. Contact us to make a request.

8. Age requirement

AI Wellness Pal is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process your data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards provided by those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the app.
